COMMUNITY STANDARDS

SERVER RULES

Great roleplay starts with respect. These are sample rules — customise them for your own community.

01 — Respect everyone

No harassment, hate speech, discrimination or bullying. Treat players and staff fairly.

02 — Stay in character

Use in-character communication during scenes. Keep out-of-character disputes out of active roleplay.

03 — No metagaming

Do not use information your character has not learned through roleplay.

04 — No powergaming

Allow others a fair chance to respond. Do not force actions or unrealistic outcomes on other players.

05 — Value your life

Respond realistically to dangerous situations and allow scenes to unfold naturally.

06 — Follow staff guidance

Use the appropriate support channel for issues and appeal decisions respectfully.
